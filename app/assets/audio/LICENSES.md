License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0).

Downloaded from the author's public high-quality MP3 preview. The author
describes this as a performed kitten vocalization, not a live kitten recording.

The WAV effects are original procedural sound designs for Anivermaru,
generated by `tool/create_game_sfx.py`: soft foil tear, glassy card reveal,
wooden placement, candy chimes, spring jump and a modulated tractor beam.
They contain no third-party samples.

Background music is provided by the project owner. The files in `music/` are grouped by game through `music/tracks.json`. Tracks rotate on completion, with pauses and crossfades when changing scenes.

## Marus vs Zombies

The prepared combat effects `marus_launcher*.wav`, `marus_harvest*.wav`,
`marus_bite*.wav`, `marus_armor*.wav` and `marus_defeat*.wav` derive from
CC0 recordings by Kenney and artisticdude. Source URLs, processing metadata
and hashes are recorded in `audio_sources/marus/manifest.json` and
`audio_sources/marus/patio_selection_v35.json`. Original bank files remain
outside the Flutter asset bundle.

`music/marus/garden-patrol.wav` is procedural music generated by
`tool/create_marus_music.py`.
`music/marus/zombies-on-your-lawn.mp3` (Laura Shigihara) and
`marus_card_victory.mp3` were supplied by the project owner; no CC0 license
is asserted for these tracks.
