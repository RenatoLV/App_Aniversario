Downloaded from the author's public high-quality MP3 preview. The author
describes this as a performed kitten vocalization, not a live kitten recording.

The WAV effects are original procedural sound designs for Anivermaru,
generated by `tool/create_game_sfx.py`: soft foil tear, glassy card reveal,
wooden placement, candy chimes, spring jump and a modulated tractor beam.
They contain no third-party samples.

Music has not been supplied yet. Put licensed/user-provided MP3, OGG or WAV
files in `music/` and map their filenames in `music/tracks.json` to menu,
blocks, wordle, sweet or leap. The app loops each track and crossfades for 700 ms.
